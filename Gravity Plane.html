Gravity Plane

Newtonian N-body sandbox on the Cartesian plane, integrated with velocity Verlet.

ai = G Σj mj (rj − ri) / (|rj − ri|² + ε²)3/2

Physics

Scenarios are tuned for G = 1. Change it mid-run and watch orbits tighten or fly apart. Negative G makes gravity repel.

Smooths the force at very short range so close passes don't blow up.

More substeps means a smaller Δt and more accurate orbits. Watch ΔE/E₀ on the plane.
